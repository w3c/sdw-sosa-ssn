Planning

Systems and platforms may be deployed with the intention of executing actuations, observations, or sampling at some future time. Two patterns are particularly useful for this purpose.

  1. A sosa:System (sosa:Actuator, sosa:Sensor, sosa:Sampler) can be attached to a sosa:Platform as part of a sosa:Deployment.

    The Deployment will usually be focussed on a specified Feature of Interest, and will be for a specified time interval. The System might be composed of sub-systems. Actuators and Sensors will each address specified Properties of the Feature of Interest.
  2. A Collection (i.e., sosa:ActuationCollection, sosa:ObservationCollection, sosa:SamplingCollection, or sosa:SampleCollection) with one or more common properties, but with no members.

    This can denote a 'potential' or 'future' collection, with subsequent individual Executions or Samples asserting that they are sosa:memberOf the pre-defined collection. These collection members must follow the consistency requirements, and will thus have the pre-defined values for the shared properties (e.g., sosa:usedProcedure, sosa:madeBySystem, sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest, sosa:actsOnProperty, sosa:observedProperty, etc). The empty collection thus defines a plan for future executions or samples.
