BFO/CCO Alignment Module

This section introduces the alignment of SSN to the Basic Formal Ontology ([[BFO]]) and Common Core Ontologies ([[CCO]]).

BFO is a 'top level ontology'. In particular, BFO makes a basic distinction between Continuants and Occurrents. This allows us to make a primary alignment of sosa:Execution and sosa:Deployment with BFO Occurrent, and sosa:System and sosa:Sample with BFO Continuant.

CCO is a 'mid level ontology', based on BFO, and providing a set of common concepts that are used in many domains.

Namespaces

The following namespace prefixes are used in the alignment of SSN to BFO and CCO.

sosa:
http://www.w3.org/ns/sosa/
obo:
http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/
cco:
https://www.commoncoreontologies.org/

Class Alignments

The primary classes from SSN are aligned with the BFO and CCO classes as follows.

The notation used in this section is described in .

The definition of each BFO/CCO concept is displayed as a tooltip when hovering over the concept.

  • sosa:Deployment ⊑ obo:BFO_0000015
    process
  • sosa:Execution ⊑ obo:BFO_0000015
    process
  • sosa:FeatureOfInterest ⊑ obo:BFO_0000001
    entity
  • sosa:Platform ⊑ obo:BFO_0000040 or cco:ont00000591
    material entity or Artifact Location
  • sosa:Procedure ⊑ cco:ont00000853
    Descriptive Information Content Entity
  • sosa:Property ⊑ obo:BFO_0000031 or obo:BFO_0000019
    generically dependent continuant or quality
  • sosa:Sample ⊑ obo:BFO_0000004
    independent continuant
  • sosa:System ⊑ obo:BFO_0000004
    independent continuant

Most of these alignments are straightforward, but a few require some explanation.

  1. A sosa:Platform may be a `material entity` such as a buoy, station, satellite, etc. Or it may just be a location without any physical infrastructure.
  2. A sosa:Procedure is a re-usable recipe or protocol or system datasheet, thus it is purely information content, in particular it describes the steps that a system must implement.
  3. A sosa:Property may be generic, so that it applies to many entities. Or it may be specific to a particular individual. See for further discussion.
