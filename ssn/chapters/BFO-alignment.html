BFO/CCO Alignment Module

This section introduces the alignment of SSN to the Basic Formal Ontology ([[BFO]]) and Common Core Ontologies ([[CCO]]).

BFO is a 'top level ontology', which makes a basic distinction between Continuants and Occurrents. This allows us to make a primary alignment of sosa:Execution and sosa:Deployment with BFO Occurrent, and sosa:System and sosa:Sample with BFO Continuant.

CCO is a 'mid level ontology', based on BFO. CCO provides modules for several sets of common concepts that may then be used in many application domains.

Namespaces

The following namespace prefixes are used in the alignment of SSN to BFO and CCO.

sosa:
http://www.w3.org/ns/sosa/
obo:
http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/
cco:
https://www.commoncoreontologies.org/

Class Alignments

The primary classes from SSN are aligned with the BFO and CCO classes as follows.

  • sosa:Actuation ≡ cco:ont00000357 or cco:ont00000908
    "Act of Motion" or "Act of Artifact Processing"
  • sosa:Deployment ⊑ cco:ont00000228
    Planned Act
  • sosa:Execution ⊑ cco:ont00000228
    Planned Act
  • sosa:FeatureOfInterest ⊑ obo:BFO_0000001
    entity
  • sosa:Observation ≡ cco:ont00000037 or cco:ont00000345 or cco:ont00000371
    "Act of Observation" or "Act of Measuring" or "Act of Prediction"
  • sosa:Platform ⊑ obo:BFO_0000040 or cco:ont00000591
    "material entity" or "Artifact Location"
  • sosa:Procedure ⊑ cco:ont00000965
    Directive Information Content Entity
  • sosa:Property ⊑ obo:BFO_0000031 or obo:BFO_0000019
    "generically dependent continuant" or "quality"
  • sosa:Sample ⊑ obo:BFO_0000004
    independent continuant
  • sosa:Sampling ⊑ cco:ont00000908 or cco:ont00000566 or cco:ont00000366
    "Act of Artifact Processing" or "Act of Artifact Employment" or "Act of Information Processing"
  • sosa:Stimulus ⊑ or cco:ont00000978
    Cause
  • sosa:System ⊑ obo:BFO_0000004
    independent continuant

Property Alignments

The following properties from SSN have simple alignments with BFO or CCO properties:

  • sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest ⊑ cco:ont00001921
    has input
  • sosa:hasResult ⊑ cco:ont00001986
    has output
  • sosa:implements ⊑ cco:ont00001920
    prescribed by
  • sosa:isSampleOf ⊑ cco:ont00001775
    is successor of
  • sosa:madeBySystem ⊑ obo:BFO_0000057
    has participant
  • sosa:usedProcedure ⊑ cco:ont00001920
    prescribed by
  • sosa:wasOriginatedBy ⊑ cco:ont00001819
    caused by

Explanations

Most of these alignments are straightforward, but a few merit some explanation.

  1. A sosa:Platform may be a `material entity` such as a buoy, station, satellite, etc. Or it may just be a location without any physical infrastructure.
  2. A sosa:Procedure is a re-usable recipe or protocol or system datasheet, thus it is purely information content. In particular it describes the actions and settings that a system must implement.
  3. A sosa:Property may be generic, so that it applies to many entities. Or it may be specific to a particular individual. See for further discussion.
  4. A sosa:Stimulus is an interaction of an observable proxy for the desired property with the sensor. See [[SSO-Pattern]] for further discussion.
  5. The range of sosa:hasResult is not specified. However, following its definition we can infer that
    • in the context of sosa:Actuation or sosa:Observation, the range of sosa:hasResult is cco:ont00000958 Information Content Entity
    • in the context of sosa:Sampling, the range of sosa:hasResult is obo:BFO_0000004 independent continuant
