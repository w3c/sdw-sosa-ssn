BFO/CCO Alignment Module

This section introduces the alignment of SOSA to the Basic Formal Ontology ([[BFO]]) and Common Core Ontologies ([[CCO]]).

BFO is a 'top level ontology'. In particular, BFO makes a basic distinction between Continuants and Occurrents. This allows us to make a primary alignment of sosa:Execution and sosa:Deployment with BFO Occurrent, and sosa:System and sosa:Sample with BFO Continuant.

CCO is a 'mid level ontology', based on BFO, and providing a set of common concepts that are used in many domains.

This section uses a combination of [[Description-Logics]] notation (see also the [[Description-Logics-Home-Page]]) and the Manchester Syntax [[owl2-manchester-syntax]].
⊑ denotes either SubClassOf or SubObjectPropertyOf, depending on the context.
≡ indicates equivalence, corresponding to either EquivalentClasses or EquivalentObjectProperties, depending on the context.
∪ denotes Union class.
The expression OP1 o OP2 ⊑ OP3 represents a subproperty chain axiom.

Namespaces

The following namespace prefixes are used in the alignment of SOSA to BFO and CCO.

sosa:
http://www.w3.org/ns/sosa/
obo:
http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/
cco:
https://www.commoncoreontologies.org/

Class Alignments

The primary classes from SSN are aligned with the BFO and CCO classes as follows.

  • sosa:Execution ⊑ obo:BFO_0000015 process
  • sosa:FeatureOfInterest ⊑ obo:BFO_0000001 entity
  • sosa:Property ⊑ obo:BFO_0000019 quality
  • sosa:Sample ⊑ obo:BFO_0000002 continuant
  • sosa:System ⊑ obo:BFO_0000004 independent continuant
  • sosa:Deployment ⊑ obo:BFO_0000015 process
  • sosa:Platform ⊑ ( obo:BFO_0000040 ∪ cco:ont00000591 ) ( Material Entity ∪ Artifact Location )
  • sosa:Procedure ⊑ cco:ont00000958 Information Content Entity

Property Alignments

The following properties from SSN have simple alignments with BFO or CCO properties:

  • sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest ⊑ obo:BFO_0000057 has participant
  • sosa:hasResult ⊑ cco:ont00001986 has output
  • sosa:implements ⊑ obo:BFO_0000034 function
  • sosa:isSampleOf ⊑ cco:ont00001775 is successor of
  • sosa:madeBySystem ⊑ obo:BFO_0000057 has participant
  • sosa:usedProcedure ⊑ obo:BFO_0000055 realizes
  • sosa:wasOriginatedBy ⊑ cco:ont00001819 caused by
