DOLCE Alignment Module

This section introduces the alignment of SSN with the DOLCE upper ontology [[DOLCE]]. This serves to axiomatically clarify the intended meaning of SSN terms and will assist SSN and SOSA users wishing to interoperate with other DOLCE-aligned ontologies. The DOLCE alignment can be used independently to align SOSA with more generic concepts/properties of DOLCE.

The ontology IRI of the DOLCE Alignment module is http://www.w3.org/ns/sosa/dolce
The version IRI of the DOLCE Alignment module 2023 edition is http://www.w3.org/ns/sosa/2023/dolce

Namespaces

The following namespace prefixes are used in the alignment of SSN with DOLCE.

sosa:
http://www.w3.org/ns/sosa/
db:
https://w3id.org/DOLCE/OWL/DOLCEbasic#

Class Alignments

The upper-level classes from SSN are aligned with the DOLCE classes as follows. The alignment of subclasses of these upper-level classes follows logically from these correspondences.

The notation used in this section is described in .

The definition of each DOLCE concept is displayed as a tooltip when hovering over the concept.

  • sosa:Asset ⊑ db:PhysicalObject or db:SocialObject
  • sosa:Deployment ⊑ db:Event and ( db:Accomplishment or db:Achievement )
  • sosa:Execution ⊑ db:Event and ( db:Accomplishment or db:Achievement )
  • sosa:ExecutionCollection ⊑ db:Perdurant
  • sosa:FeatureOfInterest ⊑ db:Endurant or db:Perdurant
  • sosa:MaterialSample ⊑ db:AmountOfMatter or db:PhysicalObject
  • sosa:Procedure ⊑ db:NonAgentiveSocialObject
  • sosa:Property ⊑ db:Quality or db:Region
  • sosa:Sample ⊑ db:Endurant or db:Perdurant
  • sosa:SampleCollection ⊑ db:Endurant or db:Perdurant
  • sosa:SpatialSample ⊑ db:Feature
  • sosa:Stimulus ⊑ db:Event and ( db:Accomplishment or db:Achievement )
