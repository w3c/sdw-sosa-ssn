Procedure definitions

The SSN Ontology does not provide a pattern for describing procedures in general.

For Samples, the structure sosa-oms:PreparationProcedure, sosa-oms:samplePreparationStep, sosa-oms:PreparationStep, sosa-oms:processingDetails is provided in the OMS Extension Module.

To describe other 'Procedures' and their steps consider using Prov-o or p-plan to define the detail. This is demonstrated in a recipe example.
