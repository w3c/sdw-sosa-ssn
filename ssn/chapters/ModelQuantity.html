Quantity Values and Units of Measure

The result of a sosa:Observation or a sosa:Actuation can be a quantity value with a numeric value and a unit of measure.

Recommending a single way of representing quantitative results is is out of scope for this Recommendation. Several options from external vocabularies are available for expressing quantities as OWL individuals, or as datatypes. The examples in this section are based on the use of `qudt:QuantityValue` from the Quantities, Units, Dimensions, and Data Types ontologies [[QUDT]]. Another option is `cdt:ucum` from Custom Datatypes [[CDT]], but use of custom datatypes requires an extension to standard RDF and OWL processors.

Collections



    In a collection of observations or actuations the members will often use a common unit of measurement.
    This can be specified at the level of the collection using
    `qudt:hasUnit`.





    If `qudt:hasUnit` is present on a collection, then the consistency rules described for
    Actuation properties or
    Observation properties apply.
